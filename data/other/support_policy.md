# Customer Support Policy

## Support Hours

Customer support is available Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM IST.

## Priority Levels

Critical issues have a target first-response time of 30 minutes.

Standard issues have a target first-response time of 4 business hours.

## Escalation

Unresolved critical issues are escalated to the support manager after 2 hours.

## Supported Channels

Customers can contact support through email and the support portal.
